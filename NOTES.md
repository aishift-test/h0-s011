- Done: landing page — all 11 sections, navy + gold, phone first, video player, join panel.
- Done: Abha guide — 18 places, 4 type filters, a SEASON filter and a month picker, a 4-slot "Plan my day" (it opens on the student's own plan from the PDF), photo credits in the footer.
- Done: the guide is in TWO LANGUAGES — English and Arabic — with a round button in the toolbar. The page keeps the SAME left-to-right shape in both languages: only the words and the font change (El Messiri for headings, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body text), so the labels stay on the left and the buttons never jump to the other side. It remembers the choice (`localStorage`, key `abha-lang-v1`). English words live in `data-en` / `data-ar` on the tags in `abha/index.html`; every sentence the page builds itself is in the `T` table in `abha/script.js`. Arabic place names are `nameAr`, `lineAr`, `tipAr`, `altAr` on each place. English stays the identity key everywhere, so saved places and the plan keep working in both.
- Done: "Plan my day" — every stop shows a clock time (8:00 am / 1:00 pm / 4:30 pm / 7:00 pm, or 8:00 ص in Arabic). Times are in `SLOTS` in `abha/script.js`. "Add this day to my calendar" builds `abha-day.ics` in the browser for the coming Friday, in Abha time (UTC+3), written as UTC. Arabic lines in the file are folded at 75 bytes so any calendar app reads them.
- Done: a small, quick settle-in as you reach each piece — the hero words one after the other, the cards, "Plan my day", the plan box, the footer. Each piece rises 14px and fades in 0.42s, and then the script takes the animation off it again, so NOTHING is left running while you scroll. The first try at this was much bigger (long slides, a progress bar, photos drifting at different speeds) and it made the student's phone slow, so it was cut right back. Please keep it this small. All of it is off for anyone who asks their computer for less movement, off on paper, and off if the script fails. The moving is the `arrive` animation in `abha/styles.css`; `abha/script.js` only says when a piece has been reached. The `js` class on `<html>` is the switch.
- Done: a heart on every card saves that place (`localStorage`, key `abha-saved-v1`) and a "Saved" tab shows them. A line above the cards reads the real month off the device: "It is November in Abha. 11 of the 18 places are at their best now."
- Done: a "Pick one for me" button beside "Places to see" picks at random from what you can see, scrolls to that card, rings it in purple, and shows a "Pick again" pill. It never picks the same place twice in a row.
- Done: every Maps link checked one by one against Google Maps. 12 of the 18 open exactly one right place; Habala opens a short list whose first result is the cable car down to the village. The last 5 are a dish or a made-up place, so their button says "Find it in Abha" instead of "Open in Maps". Add `mapsSearch: true` to a place to get that wording.

- Done: a round dark/light button beside the language button in the toolbar. It shows a moon while the page is light and a sun while it is dark. The choice is remembered (`localStorage`, key `abha-theme-v1`); if the student has never pressed it, the page follows the computer's own setting. The colours are the `DARK COLOURS` block in `abha/styles.css` — `data-theme="dark"` on `<html>` is the switch, and `index.html` sets it before the page paints so there is no white flash. Only the light parts change: the hero photo, the day planner and the footer are dark in both. Purple TEXT gets lighter in dark (`--accent`), but a purple BUTTON keeps a deep purple (`--accent-fill`) because its words are white. Printing always uses the light colours, whatever is on screen.
- Done: EVERY choice survives a refresh. The language (`abha-lang-v1`), the saved hearts (`abha-saved-v1`) and the dark/light choice (`abha-theme-v1`) always did. Added: the three rows of tabs — Show, Season, Best in (`abha-view-v1`, saved inside `render()`, put back by `loadView()` + `paintTabs()` at start) and the day on screen (`abha-plan-v1`, saved by `savePlan()` from `makePlan()` and `showMyPlan()`, put back by `loadPlan()`). A saved choice is checked against the tab names first, so an old or unknown value can never leave the page empty. NOTE: `TYPE_KEYS` / `SEASON_KEYS` are the lists of tab names — do not call either one `TYPES` or `SEASONS`, because `SEASONS` is already the table of seasons and a second `var SEASONS` silently breaks the month filter.
- Done: when nothing matches, the page says so. The old message was a thin grey line that ended up OFF THE SCREEN: an empty filter makes the page much shorter, the browser clamps the scroll position, and the visitor was left looking at blank space with no idea why. Now `#empty` is a white card — a heading, a helpful line and a "Show everything" button — and `render(asked)` only calls `empty.scrollIntoView()` when the visitor actually pressed something, so the answer is always brought into view but the page never jumps on its own at load. `render(true)` from the six press handlers, plain `render()` from `applyLang()`. With today's 18 places only the Saved tab can be empty (0 of 80 type x season/month combinations are).
